Winnow

a decoy-aware adversary defeats ghost-keystroke defenses in virtual reality

VR virtual keyboards leak what you type through your avatar's hands, and the proposed fix injects decoy "ghost" keystrokes to hide the real ones. Winnow is the adversary that calls the bluff: aware that decoys exist, it filters them and recovers the input, showing the defense class needs a stronger foundation than plausible noise.
